Manual import

Export visible bookmarks from your regular logged-in browser, then upload the JSON here. This bypasses Playwright login entirely.

Browser export steps

1. Open `x.com/i/bookmarks` in your normal browser.

2. Scroll until the bookmarks you want are visible.

3. Open DevTools Console and paste the snippet from `scripts/export_bookmarks_console.js`.

4. Save the downloaded JSON and upload it here.
